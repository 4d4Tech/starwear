# Antigravity CLI: Shop Page Voice & Context Rewrite Directive

## Executive Directive & Purpose

This document directs the copy transformation across the shopping interface for the Antigravity CLI and Star weAR storefronts.

The primary goal is to strip away clinical tech jargon and high-altitude abstractions in favor of approachable, grounded language that connects directly with everyday people. The copy must frame the underlying WebAR and routing architecture not as a cold piece of software, but as a living medium through which wearers and brands unlock boundary-free self-expression.

---

## 1. Tone, Voice & Linguistic Constraints

### Tone Balance: Grounded Clarity $\times$ High-Agency Expression

* **Approachability First:** Speak directly to the person wearing the clothes or running the label. Use straightforward analogies (e.g., permanent ink on cotton vs. a living, swappable canvas).


* **No Marketing Fluff or Gimmicks:** Avoid corporate AR buzzwords ("revolutionary metaverse integration," "disruptive apparel synergy"). Let tangible real-world freedom do the talking.


* **Strict Lexical Constraint:** **Do NOT use the word "tool."** Frame the technology as an **open canvas**, a **living frequency**, an **active medium**, an **open foundation**, or a **liberated chassis**.



### Core Metaphor Shifts

| Traditional / Corporate Term | Outdated Technical Metaphor | Grounded Expression Replacement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shirt / Hoodie / Merch | Finished Garment | **Physical Chassis / Open Canvas / Fabric Foundation**<br> |
| AR Asset / 3D Model | Digital Soul / Hologram | **Dynamic Skin / Living Visual / Swappable Frequency**<br> |
| AR Filter / Utility | Digital Tool / Gimmick | **Living Medium / Creative Freedom / Personal Channel**<br> |
| App Installation | Native Mobile Client | **Zero-Friction Web Delivery / Browser-Native Magic**<br> |

### The CLI Accent Pattern

Command-line syntax (`$ antigravity ...`) must ground the high-tech foundation without confusing non-technical shoppers. Use short, clean monospace cues paired with affirmative plain-English translations.

## 2. Core Narrative Pillars

### Pillar I: The Living Canvas (Wearer Connection)

* **Core Insight:** Permanent graphics lock a person into who they were the day they bought the shirt. Antigravity keeps the garment open, letting wearers change their visual presence as quickly as their mood changes.


* **Linguistic Cue:** "One shirt. Endless expressions." "Never wear the same static design twice."



### Pillar II: Direct Brand Sovereignty (Creator & Merchant Connection)

* **Core Insight:** Independent labels and artists are no longer trapped by supply chain lead times or app store middle-men. A line can be re-released or updated straight onto garments already out in the street.


* **Linguistic Cue:** "The point of sale is the starting line." "Broadcast your vision directly to the street."



### Pillar III: Frictionless Accessibility (Kenosis Principle)

* **Core Insight:** No apps to download, no accounts to register just to view art. Anyone can point their phone camera at the garment to watch it come alive instantly in the web browser.


* **Linguistic Cue:** "No app stores. No hurdles. Point your camera and watch your style unfold."



### Pillar IV: Zero Guesswork Previews & Bespoke Autonomy

* **Core Insight:** Eliminates online shopping doubt through real-time mirror previews, and honors individual identity via the Asterisk (`*`) protocol whenever custom artwork overrides mass templates.


* **Linguistic Cue:** "See how it drapes and folds before you commit." "Your bespoke aesthetic overrides the crowd."

## 4. Copywriter Verification Gate

Before committing changes to the storefront or CLI help dialogues, verify against these four rules:

1. **The "Tool" Prohibition:** Confirm that the word "tool" appears nowhere in user-facing copy. Substitute with words like *medium*, *canvas*, *foundation*, or *frequency*.


2. **The "Kenosis" Check:** Ensure no instruction requires or suggests downloading an app from the App Store or Google Play; the experience must remain entirely browser-native.


3. **The Expression Balance:** Verify that both the wearer's daily autonomy and the brand's ability to update designs dynamically are clearly represented.


4. **Relatability Test:** Read the copy aloud. If a sentence sounds like an enterprise software sales deck or an academic paper, rewrite it into everyday conversational language.
